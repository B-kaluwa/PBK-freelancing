Who We Are

PBK Freelancing is a premier freelancing service provider specializing in web development, cybersecurity, software development, and IT consultation. Founded by Prince B. Kaluwa, we are dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and innovative solutions to clients worldwide.


Our Mission

To empower businesses and individuals with cutting-edge technology solutions that drive growth, enhance security, and streamline operations. We strive to be the most trusted partner in digital transformation.


Our Vision

To become a global leader in freelancing services, setting new standards for quality, innovation, and client satisfaction in the digital age.

Meet the Founder

Prince B. Kaluwa

Founder & Lead Developer

Prince B. Kaluwa is a passionate technologist with extensive experience in web development, cybersecurity, and software engineering. With a vision to provide exceptional freelancing services, he founded PBK Freelancing to help businesses leverage technology for growth and success.
